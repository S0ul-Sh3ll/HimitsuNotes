# Privacy Policy — Himitsu Notes

**Last updated:** 2 September 2026

Himitsu Notes ("the app") is a private, offline notes application. This policy explains what
data the app does and does not handle.

## Data we collect

**None.** Himitsu Notes does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- All notes, checklists, tables, images, attachments, labels, reminders, PINs and settings are
  stored **only on your device**.
- Note content is encrypted at rest using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM (Android Keystore).
- The app has **no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no telemetry**.
- No note content ever leaves your device.

## Network use

The app requests the `INTERNET` permission for a single purpose: to communicate with **Google
Play Billing** when you choose to make the optional one-time "Pro" purchase. This communication
is handled by Google Play; the app does not send any of your notes or personal information.

Purchase processing is subject to
[Google Play's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions

- `INTERNET` — Google Play Billing (Pro purchase) only.
- `USE_BIOMETRIC` — optional biometric unlock, performed on-device.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` / `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `VIBRATE` — local reminder notifications.

## Data deletion

Because nothing is collected or stored off-device, uninstalling the app permanently removes all
of your data from the device. There is no server-side data to delete.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
